Focus Mode

Show only videos that match your topics, allowed keywords or allowed channels.

Mode

Shortcut: Alt+Shift+F

Off-topic videos in feeds

Apply the filter to

Includes search results, so searching for something off-topic doesn't get around Focus Mode. Untick Search to leave results unfiltered.

Guard off-topic videos and blocked searches

Pause and ask before playing an off-topic or blocked video, and before showing results for a search that matches your blocklist. Videos opened from a channel or playlist play normally.

Topics

A video matches a topic when its title or channel contains the topic name or any of its keywords.

Privacy & data

  • Settings and statistics are stored only in this browser, with chrome.storage.local.
  • FocusTube does not record your browsing or watch history. Statistics are counts only.
  • FocusTube makes no network requests and sends nothing to any server.
  • No account is needed. The only site it runs on is youtube.com.
